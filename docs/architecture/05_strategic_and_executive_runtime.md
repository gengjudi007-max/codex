# 05 Strategic and Executive Runtime

## Strategic Intelligence Runtime

The strategic runtime upgrades realtime newsroom events into long-cycle industry intelligence.

Core capabilities:

- market regime detection
- policy semantic evolution
- land finance analysis
- developer balance-sheet repair analysis
- property-service restructuring analysis
- city inventory and acquisition tracking
- strategic storyline generation
- risk propagation tracking

## Executive Intelligence Runtime

The executive layer acts as a chief-editor and think-tank system.

Core capabilities:

- scenario planning
- risk path forecast
- narrative competition analysis
- editorial positioning
- policy transmission modeling
- chief-editor brief
- institutional memory updates

## Runtime Flow

```text
new source
  ↓
connector event
  ↓
knowledge graph update
  ↓
autonomous intelligence
  ↓
strategic intelligence
  ↓
executive intelligence
  ↓
editorial decisions
```

## Strategic Principles

1. Do not treat short-term transaction recovery as automatic cycle reversal.
2. Separate policy intention from actual market transmission.
3. Evaluate companies through cash flow, debt, and balance-sheet repair.
4. Track city differentiation instead of using nationwide averages only.
5. Treat land finance as a structural driver of local market behavior.

## Executive Outputs

The runtime should continuously generate:

- strategic storylines
- weekly executive briefs
- policy watchlists
- contradiction warnings
- long-cycle observations
- institutional memory updates
